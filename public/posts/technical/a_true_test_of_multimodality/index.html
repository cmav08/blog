I carried out a test for multimodality across four different platforms, Chatgpt, Claude, Gemini and Grok.

In this post we will rank them based on their ability to correctly identify each character featured in this AOT season 4 poster.

The criterias which are going to be used are:

  1. Accurate Chartacter Identification: This measures the number of characters identified along with their accurate naming descriptions
  2. Accurate descriptions: This include descriptions of position, posture, expressions and physical features.

Both of these helps us to identify how wel these models are able to provide the releevant contexxt and what they halluxinate about.

  1. ChatGPT: Overview While Chatgpt was able to accurately identify most of the characters in the poster it made crucial mistakes in analyzing the position, posture and context of the characters in the image

  2. Accurate Character Description: Good:

  • Correctly identified the characters and the context of: All the titans, Falco and Gabi, Jean, Levi, Armin and Mikasa Bad:
  • Incorrectly identified Erwin smith even though he wasn’t in the poster and contextuallly not part of the event
  • Missed Connie Springer and Hange Zoe as being part of the image
  1. Accurate Descriptioms

Good

  • Was able to accurately describe the facial expressions of Gabi and falco
  • Correctly Identified the position in which Mikasa was flying (i.e. Diagonally Forward)

Bad

  • Incorrecly Hallucinated that Mikasa was wearing the brown scarf which she usually wore throughout the series
  • Incorrectly identified that Levi Ackerman appeared twice in the poster.

  1. Claude: Overview: Claude’s helpfulness in this task was lackluster with it providing neither enough context or character description, and hallucinating the most of all four platforms.

  2. Accurate Character Identification:

Good

  • Correctly identifies Levi Ackerman, Mikasa, Connnie Springer, Jean Kirchstein, Sasha Blouse and Armin Arlet.

Bad

  • Incorrectly identified Jean Kirsten as Erwin Smith
  • Incorrectly identifies that the War-hammer titan is Reiner Braun (Armoured Titan)
  • Incorrectly identifies that the Jaw titan is the war hammer titan
